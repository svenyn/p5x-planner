What the Teams fight leaves out

Every effect in a character's kit that the planner does not price, collected from the four places the code records them: the "not priced" notes on each teammate, the same notes on each damage dealer, Wonder's Persona and weapon notes, and the warnings on the rotation rules. Notes that only say "this is a self-buff, already counted", "healing", "shield" or "skill level-up, covered by the tiers" are left out, because they change nothing.

The grades on the largest items are measured (below). The rest are a judgement, not a measurement: how much the Teams number for that character's teams would move if the item were priced, read from the size the kit text gives, how often it would be up, and how much of the damage factor it would land in (a +20% damage line lands in a sum that is already around 2.5×, so it moves the total by roughly 7%; a separate multiplier such as final damage or Skill Amplification moves it almost one for one). Build scores barely move for any of these: they only read a kit through its fight alone.

LargeLikely more than 10% on the teams it is in, or a whole damage source missing.
MediumRoughly 3–10%, or large but only at a high Awareness or in some teams.
SmallUnder about 3%, or rare.
Not in default fightZero against the default boss (one foe, every hit on a weakness, cannot be knocked down, HP not tracked); real under other settings.
Outside the numberDamage by someone other than the damage dealer. The team number never counts it, by design.

Notes in the code that are out of date

Found while collecting these. Each note still says "not priced" but the model has since moved on, so the note itself should be updated; they are not counted above.

  • Fuuka, Global 4.12 buff. The skip note says the calculator "should scale them"; her Radiant Power, Holy Prayer and Spirit Wave lines already carry the ×1.2 (×4/3).
  • Elemental ailments as enablers (Ann's Rosethorn and Trifire Burn, Ryuji's Shock, Morgana's Windswept, Motoha's Shock, Lufel's Burn). The fight now tracks Burn, Freeze, Shock and Windswept for 31 characters; what is still missing is only a reader for some of them (listed per character).
  • Pixie and Seth say "Down is not modelled". Knockdowns are played out when the boss is given Down Points; these two Persona lines were just never entered.
  • Ichigo's Melting Ichi-Go Butter and Runa's Resonance damage-taken lines say there is no key for them. dotDm and resDm exist now, so both can be entered.
  • Starlight Morgana, Not Done Dancing Yet (A4). Its new +10% lines are permanent from the first Highlight; the note's "only the 3-turn version" now applies only to the Highlight's original 29.3–34.1% debuffs.
